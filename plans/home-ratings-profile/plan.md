# Home, Ratings and Profile

**Status:** active

## Goal

A player opens the app to see their code, their ratings and what is happening with their matches. Home shows those three, top to bottom. Ratings is a tab of its own, with the tiles and the full history. Profile is the settings page: the code on one line, then the settings. The player's numbers no longer sit between settings, and every pending match ends up in one list on Home.

## Decisions

- #307: Home is the code strip with Copy code, then Ratings, then In progress. Recent matches and the Record a match button are gone from Home. Record stays in the nav, and the empty state for a player with no matches keeps its own Record a match button.
- #307: until #305 lands, In progress holds what Home had: the Needs you list (3 rows, then "See all N") and the counts line ("4 disputed · 2 on hold"), with the info popover after the title. Needs you is a group label (`h3`, `text-base font-semibold text-secondary`) inside the section, not a section of its own, and no longer shows its own count: its rows, or "See all N", give it, and the nav badge says it too.
- #307: the `aside` of In progress is the player's match count, "28 matches", linking to Matches. It replaces "All matches" and Profile's Matches section. It reads the count of the same history page Home already loads, so it costs no extra request. `countMatches` in `features/matches` has no caller left in the app, but stays with its tests.
- #307: the code, the ratings and the matches load on their own. If the match history fails, its error stands where In progress would be, below the code and the ratings.
- #307: the code strip moved from `features/matches` to `features/players` (`CodeStrip`), because Home and Profile both use it. It has a line above, like a section, and none below, because the Ratings or Name and privacy line follows 12 below. Profile's code card is gone, and with it the Share button. The issue asks for the code with Copy, and Share only ever showed on devices with a share sheet.
- #307: Profile has no name line under the title. The display name field shows the name. (Superseded by #328: the name can't be edited, and Account shows it as a text row.)
- #307: Profile's sections are Name and privacy, Two-factor authentication (the Security group from #301), Your data, then Account. "Your data" is its own component (`YourData`), so the route can put two-factor before it. The two-factor section keeps its title.
- #307: the Ratings tab is `/ratings` (`ROUTES.ratings`), between Matches and Profile, with the Lucide `chart-line` icon. Its page title is "Ratings", then "Current ratings" (the tiles) and "Rating history" (the list from #288, 5 to a page). It has no back link, since it is a tab. The history list drops its top border, because it sits right under its section title.
- #307: on the Ratings tab the demonstration sentence is a boxed notice (`Alert`, default variant, with the `info` icon and the title "Demonstration numbers"), not a plain line, because a tab of its own makes the numbers look more official. Every tile and history row keeps its Demonstration badge. Home keeps the plain sentence. The notice shows when the ratings are demonstration ones, as the sentence did.
- #307: Home's rating tiles are compact: the format, the number and the Demonstration badge, as the agreed mock showed. With every reliability line they were tall on a 375px phone and pushed In progress below the fold. Home keeps the demonstration sentence and the Details link. The full tiles, with the reliability lines, are on the Ratings tab, and so is the reliability popover from #285, after the Current ratings title. Home shows no reliability, so it has nothing to explain.
- #307: `RatingsCard` takes `title`, `details` (the "Details" link to Ratings), `compact` (Home's short tiles, with no popover) and `prominent` (the boxed notice, on Ratings).
- #307: `/profile/ratings` redirects to `/ratings` with a 308 from its `+page.ts`. Signed out, the guard sends the player to sign in first. `/ratings` is a guarded page.
- #307: `ProfileStats` became `MyRatings`, which loads only the current ratings, for Home and the Ratings tab. `HomeView` takes it as an optional prop, so its tests pass a stand-in without importing another feature's services.
- #307: five tabs fit a 320px phone: each label on one line, each tab at least 44px wide and tall, and Home's badge inside its tab. An e2e test checks this at 320 and 375. An organizer member has six (Events before Profile, `grid-cols-6`). At 320px those six still fit on one line each, about 53px a tab, with the labels close together.
- #307: a Demonstration badge (about 123px) is wider than half a 320px phone's row, so even compact tiles spilled over their edge there. The tile grid is now a container (`@container`) and takes one column below Tailwind's `@xs` (320px of room), two from there and three from `sm`. So at 320px the tiles stack, on Home and on the Ratings tab, and nothing reaches a tile's edge. From 375px they sit two to a row, as before. This settles the tight tiles from #288, so there is no follow-up.
- #319: Home and Matches keep their scroll position with SvelteKit's `snapshot` helper from `$app/navigation` (`keepScroll` in `features/matches`). The position goes into the history entry when the player leaves, and on a back or forward visit the page scrolls there again once its list has rendered. SvelteKit restores the scroll at once, while the list is still loading, so on its own it lands near the top. Rendering from the cache at once was the other choice, but each screen loads several lists, and the cache keeps matches by code, not pages, so it would need a page cache as well.
- #319: Home waits for its matches and its ratings before it scrolls back, because the rating tiles sit above In progress and change its height. Matches waits for the list of the filter and page in the address.
- #319: a new history entry, such as a nav tap, a link, or Newer and Older, has nothing to restore and starts at the top as before. The helper replaces `export const snapshot` from a page, which this SvelteKit version marks deprecated.
- #318: the feedback button is `FeedbackButton` in `ui/feedback/`, not a feature, because it calls no API. The side nav (in `ui`) and Profile both use it. It sits at the side nav foot, above Sign out, as a ghost button with the Lucide `message-square-text` icon, and in a "Feedback" section on Profile, after Your data and before Account. On Profile it shows at every width, so a desktop Profile has it twice (the section and the side nav), unlike Sign out. It is a settings row, and #271 moves it into Help and about.
- #318: the dialog is centered on phones too, with 16px either side and above and below at most, like the site's Tally popup, not a bottom sheet. The form is one field and a button, so it fits a 375px phone with room to spare. It is the shadcn-svelte Dialog with a close button (the app's other dialogs have Cancel instead), 500px wide from `md`.
- #318: Tally's form keeps its own white background in both themes (no `transparentBackground=1`). Its text colors are fixed by the form, so on a transparent background it would be dark text on black. The site's popup is white as well.
- #318: the iframe has a fixed height (`h-72`), because Tally's automatic height needs its `embed.js`. The form fits it at 375px and 500px. A longer form scrolls inside the iframe.
- #318: the form counts as loaded when Tally's embed posts `Tally.FormLoaded` to the page (the message its own `embed.js` listens for), checked by origin and by the iframe's window. Until then the dialog shows "Loading the form". Offline when it opens, offline while loading, or no message in 10 seconds: the iframe goes and the dialog says the form didn't load, with a link that opens `https://tally.so/r/EqWB5B` in a new tab. If Tally ever stops sending the message, players get the link after 10 seconds instead of the form.
- #318: the e2e tests stand in for tally.so with a small page that posts the same message, so they never reach Tally and never submit the real form. The real form was checked by hand in the mock build: it loads under the sandbox and `no-referrer`, takes text, and Tally's own page view request to `api.tally.so` goes out from inside the sandbox, which is how a submission is sent too. It was never submitted.
- #271: Profile is the code line, then four sections (Account, Privacy, Your data, Help and about), then Sign out. Each section is a `Section` with a list of shadcn-svelte Item rows (`ui/item/`). A row that opens a page ends in a Lucide `chevron-right`, a row that opens openpb.org in a new tab ends in `external-link`. The rows have no leading icons.
- #271: Item was added with the CLI and restyled: only Item, Group, Content, Title, Description and Actions are kept (no Header, Footer, Media or Separator). Group is a `<ul>` with a line between rows and none above or below, so each row sits in an `<li>` and a screen reader counts them. Rows dim on hover like the ghost and link buttons, and are at least 56px tall (`min-h-14`). The CLI also tried to change `tailwind-variants` and add `@internationalized/date`; both were left as they were.
- #271: the rows today: Account > Display name (`/profile/name`, removed by #328, which shows the name as a text row) and Two-factor authentication (`/profile/two-factor`, with "On" or "Off" when known); Privacy > Profile visibility (`/profile/privacy`, the existing switches, unchanged; #328 adds the last name switch as its first row); Your data > Copy of your data (`/profile/data`, the existing note on how to ask for a copy); Help and about > Send feedback (the #318 dialog), Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, App version. There is no password row, because changing a password while signed in does not exist yet (#275).
- #271: Terms and policies is two rows, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, each a link straight to `TERMS_URL` or `PRIVACY_URL` in a new tab, rather than one row that opens a page with two links.
- #271: each settings page under Profile uses `SettingsPage` (`features/players`): a back link to Profile, then the title. The two-factor page uses it too. The back link helper in `features/matches` remembers where a match page came from, which these pages don't need.
- #271: `ProfileSettings` became `NameSettings` (removed by #328) and `VisibilitySettings`, which share `SettingsLoader` (the load, the loading, error and not-available states). Each settings page puts them inside `OwnCodeGate`, so a fresh visit reads `me/player` twice (the code, then the settings) and Profile reads it once.
- #271: `TwoFactorStatus` is gone: the Account row says on or off, and the organizer callout sits at the top of Account, above the rows.
- #271: `FeedbackButton` takes a `trigger` snippet, so Profile's row is the dialog's opener. The side nav keeps its button.
- #271: Sign out stays hidden on Profile from `lg`, as before, because the side nav foot has it. So there is one Sign out on screen at every width.
- #271: the App version is `version` from `package.json` and the short commit, set by the `open-app-version` plugin in `vite.config.ts` (`__APP_VERSION__`, `__APP_COMMIT__`). The commit is `APP_COMMIT` when the host sets it, otherwise git's `HEAD`, otherwise left out, and the row then shows the version alone. Only a hex hash is used. The values are plain text in the bundle, so the content security policy is unchanged.
