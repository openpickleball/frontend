About OPEN

A pickleball rating anyone can check.

OPEN, the Open Pickleball Evaluation Network, is a free, open-source rating network that no company owns. We are building it in public, and we need players, organizers, and builders to help shape it.

See how you can help

Early development. No OPEN rating is live yet.

A rating decides which events you can enter, who you get matched with, and how you see your own progress. Something with that much influence should be open to inspection.

  • See it.

    Every rating will come with a clear explanation of how it was calculated.

  • Question it.

    When a number looks wrong, you will be able to challenge it and the method behind it.

  • Take it with you.

    You will be able to export your record and bring it to a different club, league, or app.

OPEN works alongside the ratings you already have, so nobody has to choose. Apps, leagues, clubs, and tournaments are partners, and anything they build can use OPEN for free.

01 · Promises

Five promises, written into a charter

Everyone who takes part agrees to the mission charter (opens in a new tab). It can be clarified or strengthened. It can't be weakened.

Free
No subscriptions, paid tiers, advertisements, or premium features, ever.
Transparent
Open-source code and a published, versioned rating spec.
Community-run
No person, company, or sponsor owns or controls it, including the founder.
Portable data
Players and leagues can export their data at any time.
One standard
Anyone can read and fork the code, but only the governing board changes the official spec, and only conforming tools can show an OPEN rating.

Players never pay

  • Ratings are free forever.
  • Players are never asked for money, in the app or at events.
  • Donors, grant makers, and sponsors get no say over ratings.
  • Every dollar in and out is published.
  • No advertisements.

02 · Get involved

OPEN needs you, and you don't need to write code

It's early, so your input can shape what gets built. You don't need to apply or join the organization. Pick the line that sounds like you. The links open on GitHub, which needs a free account.

  • Players

    Tell us what you need from a rating and what would make one easier to trust.

    Send feedback (opens in a new tab)

  • Clubs and organizers

    Share how you run events and record results.

    Share how you run events (opens in a new tab)

  • App and league builders

    Tell us what you'd need to send results to OPEN or show OPEN ratings in your product.

    Send feedback (opens in a new tab)

  • Statisticians and rating enthusiasts

    Review the methods, challenge the assumptions, and bring evidence for a better spec.

    Propose a spec change (opens in a new tab)

  • Developers and designers

    Help build the engine, APIs, and tools. The repositories are new, so early contributors help set their direction.

    Read the contributing guide (opens in a new tab)

Not sure where you fit? Ask a question on GitHub Discussions (opens in a new tab). OPEN is run by volunteers, so replies may take a few days. Please keep real player names, contact details, and match histories out of public posts.
