What OPEN is

OPEN (Open Pickleball Evaluation Network) is a free pickleball rating system, built in the open by volunteers. Its interim steward runs it until the governing board is seated. In these terms, "OPEN", "we" and "us" mean the project.

These terms cover the OPEN service: this website, openpb.org, and the OPEN app, which will be at app.openpb.org. By using either, or an OPEN account, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't use OPEN.

Matches

This applies once player accounts open.

  • Only submit matches that really happened, with the true scores.
  • The other players in a match can confirm it or dispute it. A disputed match doesn't count while it is disputed.
  • While a match is disputed, you can withdraw your dispute. You can dispute a match only once, so you can't dispute it again after you withdraw.
  • For a match that isn't from an event, the players settle it. While the match waits for confirmation or is disputed, a player in it can correct it, with the same teams. The correction counts once the other team confirms it.
  • For a match from an event, the event's verified organizer settles a dispute. It can confirm the match, void it or correct it.
  • A dispute nobody settles in 30 days voids the match.
  • If two matches look like the same match, neither counts until each one is marked as a real, separate match: by a player on each team, or by the organizer for a match from an event. If that hasn't happened after 14 days and the earlier match is confirmed, the newer one is voided.
  • You can report an event's organizer to OPEN, for example if it gets results wrong or ignores disputes. A report doesn't change any match by itself.
  • OPEN's moderators decide whether an organizer stays verified. They can remove an organizer's verified status. They can also void at once all the matches a person or app submitted, if those break these terms.
  • OPEN keeps a record of every change to a match.

Ratings

No OPEN rating is published yet. Once ratings are published, a rating is an estimate based on match results. It is not a guarantee of how well you play. Organizers decide who can enter their events.

Fair play and conduct

When you use OPEN, please don't:

  • Submit fake matches, or arrange matches to push a rating up or down.
  • Pretend to be someone else.
  • Harass or bully other players.
  • Try to break or overload OPEN, or get around its limits.
  • Collect other players' data.

Everyone in OPEN follows our Code of Conduct (opens in a new tab).
