Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective November 23, 2026

Player accounts are not open yet. Today, only the part about this website applies. The parts about accounts say what will happen once accounts open.

The short version

  • OPEN is free. There are no ads, and we never sell your data.
  • This website has no tracking and no analytics.
  • We collect only what we need to record your matches and run OPEN.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account, at any time.

Who we are

OPEN (Open Pickleball Evaluation Network) is a volunteer project. Its interim steward runs it until the governing board is seated. In this policy, "OPEN", "we" and "us" mean the project.

Questions about your privacy go to info@openpb.org.

This website, openpb.org

This part applies today.

  • Hosting. GitHub Pages hosts openpb.org. When you visit, GitHub receives your IP address and details about your browser, as any web server does, and keeps them in its server logs. GitHub's privacy statement (opens in a new tab) covers that.
  • Fonts. The fonts come from openpb.org itself, so no font service learns about your visit.
  • Theme. If you pick the black or the ivory theme, your browser saves that choice in its local storage. It stays on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • Cookies and analytics. OPEN sets no cookies on this website and runs no analytics.
  • The "Get involved" form. The home page loads a script from Tally, the service that runs our "Get involved" form. What you enter in the form goes to Tally and to us. We use it only to reply to you and to send you the OPEN updates you asked for. Tally's privacy policy (opens in a new tab) covers how Tally handles it. To be taken off our list, email info@openpb.org.

Player accounts

This part applies once player accounts open.

What you give us

  • Email address, to sign in and to get account email, such as the message that confirms your address.
  • Password. We store only a salted hash of it, never the password itself.
  • Birth month and year, never the day. We use them for age groups, age divisions and the rule that players must be 13 or older.
  • Gender division: men's or women's. It decides whether you play in men's or women's doubles, and it is used for mixed doubles.
  • Your name. A display name, which other players see. You can also add your legal name. We use it only for identity checks and to verify organizers.
  • City and region, if you add them. They help players tell apart people with the same name, and find players near them.

Location search at signup

When you type your location at signup, the app sends what you type to Photon, a place search service built on OpenStreetMap and run by komoot. Photon suggests places to pick from. We store only the city and region you pick.

What OPEN records

  • Your OPEN Code, a public ID for you inside OPEN. A code is never given to anyone else.
  • Your matches: who played, on which team, the scores, the date and the place. The place is a name, such as a court or a park, never a street address.
  • Whether you confirmed or disputed a match, and when.
  • Your privacy settings.
  • Your sign-in session, until you log out or it expires. Also when you joined and when you last signed in.
  • A record of changes, such as who confirmed, voided or corrected a match. These records never hold your name, email address, age or other details that identify you.
  • To stop abuse, sign-in and signup limits keep your IP address in short-term memory, only for as long as the limit lasts.

Who can see what

Profiles are only for signed-in players. Visitors who are not signed in can't see them. "Staff" below means OPEN volunteers whose role needs the information.

  • Display name and OPEN Code: every signed-in player, including in player search. While an account is inactive, its matches show "Private player" instead, and player search leaves it out.
  • Your matches: you see all of yours. The players in a match see it, with each player shown by display name and OPEN Code. Other signed-in players see your confirmed matches in your match history, as long as your match history setting allows it. A verified organizer of the event a match was part of can also see it.
  • City and region: whoever your privacy setting allows, either other signed-in players or only you.
  • Birth month and year: you, and staff. Other players see only an age group.
  • Gender division: not shown on your profile. But a rating in men's or women's doubles shows which division you play in.
  • Legal name: you, and staff.
  • Email address: you, and staff. Never other players.
  • Ratings: no OPEN rating is published yet. Once ratings are published, your display name and current ratings will be visible to every signed-in player, and you won't be able to hide them.

Partner apps

Approved partner apps, such as club or tournament software, may send in match results for a club that has allowed them to. We don't share player data with partners today. We will update this policy before we do. We will never share the identity or rating of a player aged 13 to 17 with a partner.

Services we use

These companies handle data for us, only to run OPEN:

  • GitHub hosts this website. Privacy statement (GitHub, opens in a new tab)
  • Tally runs the "Get involved" form. Privacy policy (Tally, opens in a new tab)
  • DigitalOcean runs the servers and the database behind player accounts. Privacy policy (DigitalOcean, opens in a new tab)
  • Resend sends account email. It keeps a copy of each message for up to 30 days. We keep its open and click tracking turned off. Privacy policy (Resend, opens in a new tab)
  • Photon, by komoot, suggests places when you type your location at signup. Privacy policy (komoot, opens in a new tab)

These companies may handle your data in the United States, or in other countries where they run their services.

Ages

  • You must be 13 or older to have an account.
  • Signup asks for your birth month and year first. If you are under 13, no account is made, and we keep no age or gender details. We keep only a note, in that browser session, that signup was refused.
  • For players aged 13 to 17, the privacy settings start at "only you", and the player can widen them later. Their data is never shared with partners until they turn 18.

What we never collect

  • Payment details. OPEN never charges anyone.
  • Your street address or exact location.
  • Your full birth date.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your gender identity. We only ask which division you play in.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account details until your account is deleted.
  • Match results stay in the Ledger, OPEN's permanent record of matches, even after you leave. Other players' records and ratings depend on them.
  • We haven't decided yet exactly how a deleted player's matches will be shown. We will update this policy before account deletion is available.
  • We delete each account email from our outbox once it is sent, and never keep one more than three days.

Getting a copy, or deleting your account

You can always have a copy of your data. Buttons to download your data or delete your account are not built yet. Until they are, email info@openpb.org and we will help.

Keeping your data safe

  • openpb.org and OPEN accounts use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • The codes or links we email you to confirm your address or reset your password are signed, not stored.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a security problem, please tell us at info@openpb.org.

Your choices

  • Use your privacy settings to choose who sees your city and region and your match history.
  • Change your details in your profile.
  • Ask us anything about your data at info@openpb.org.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we post the new version here with a new version number and date. If you have an account, we will email you before a change that matters to you takes effect.

Contact

Email info@openpb.org. See also our Terms of Service.
