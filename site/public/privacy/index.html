Privacy Policy

Version 2 · Effective November 24, 2026

Player accounts are not open yet. Today, only the part about this website applies. The parts about accounts say what will happen once accounts open.

The short version

  • OPEN is free. There are no ads, and we never sell your data.
  • OPEN adds no tracking or analytics of its own.
  • We collect only what we need to record your matches and run OPEN.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data at any time.

Who we are

OPEN (Open Pickleball Evaluation Network) is a volunteer project. Its interim steward runs it until the governing board is seated. In this policy, "OPEN", "we" and "us" mean the project.

This policy covers this website, openpb.org, and the OPEN app, which will be at app.openpb.org. Questions about your privacy go to info@openpb.org.

This website, openpb.org

This part applies today.

  • Hosting. GitHub Pages hosts openpb.org. When you visit, GitHub receives your IP address and details about your browser, as any web server does, and keeps them in its server logs. GitHub's privacy statement (opens in a new tab) covers that.
  • Fonts. The fonts come from openpb.org itself, so no font service learns about your visit.
  • Theme. If you pick the black or the ivory theme, your browser saves that choice in its local storage. It stays on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • Cookies and analytics. OPEN sets no cookies on this website and adds no analytics of its own.
  • The "Get involved" form. The home page loads a script from Tally, the service that runs our "Get involved" form. The script loads on every visit to the home page, so Tally receives your IP address and browser details even if you never open the form. What you enter in the form goes to Tally and to us. We use it only to reply to you and to send you the OPEN updates you asked for. Tally's privacy policy (opens in a new tab) covers how Tally handles it. To be taken off our list, email info@openpb.org.

Player accounts

This applies once player accounts open.

What you give us

  • Email address, to sign in and to get account email, such as the message that confirms your address.
  • Password. We store only a salted hash of it, never the password itself.
  • Birth month and year, never the day. We use them for age groups, age divisions and the rule that players must be 13 or older.
  • Gender division: men's or women's. It decides which doubles rating formats you can play in: men's or women's doubles, and mixed doubles.
  • Your name. Signup asks for your first and last name. We store them together as your legal name. Your starting display name is your first and last name if you are 18 or older, or your first name and last initial if you are 13 to 17. Other signed-in players see your display name, so it shows your name, or part of it, until you change it. You can change your display name later. We use your legal name only for identity checks and to verify organizers.
  • City and region, if you add them. They help players tell apart people with the same name, and find players near them.

Location search at signup

When you type your location at signup, the app sends what you type to Photon, as you type, even if you don't pick a place. OPEN never receives what you type. Photon is a public place search service built on OpenStreetMap and run by komoot. It suggests places to pick from. Like any web server, it may also receive your IP address and browser or device details. Photon is not run for OPEN, and komoot's privacy policy (opens in a new tab) covers it. We store only the city and region you pick.

What OPEN records

  • Your OPEN Code, a public ID for you inside OPEN. A code is never given to anyone else.
  • Your matches: who played and on which team, the scores, the date and time, the place, the event or club, any event or bracket labels, and who submitted the match. For the place, OPEN asks for a name, such as a court or a park, not an address.
  • Whether you confirmed or disputed a match, and when.
  • Your privacy settings: who can see your match history, stats, rating history, home club, and city and region.
  • Your sign-in session, until you log out or it expires. It also holds the email address a login was started with, how and when you signed in, and, after signup, your signup details (birth month and year, gender division, name, and city and region, if you added them) until your player profile is created, you log out, or the session ends.
  • When you joined, and when you last signed in.
  • A record of changes, such as who confirmed, voided or corrected a match. These records never hold your name, email address, age or other details that identify you. One exception: the admin history that staff see records an account by its email address.
  • To stop abuse, sign-in and signup limits keep your IP address in short-term memory, only for as long as the limit lasts.

If you run events or act for a club

  • An organizer's name. It may be a person's name.
  • Which accounts act for an organizer or a club, and in which role, such as admin or match manager.
  • The events an organizer ran, and when.
  • Whether an organizer is verified, when, and which staff account verified it.
  • For a partner app, the account that registered it.

Who can see what

This applies once player accounts open. Profiles are only for signed-in players. Visitors who are not signed in can't see them. "Staff" below means OPEN volunteers, including moderators, whose role needs the information.

  • Display name and OPEN Code: every signed-in player, including in player search. While an account is inactive, its matches show "Private player" instead, and player search leaves it out.
  • Your matches: you see all of yours. A match names every player in it by display name and OPEN Code. So a match you played also shows in your teammates' and opponents' match histories, on their own privacy settings, whatever your own setting is. Other signed-in players see your confirmed matches in your own match history while your match history setting allows it. A person who acts for the verified organizer of the event can also see a match from that event when they confirm it. So can staff and moderators whose role needs it.
  • City and region: whoever your privacy setting allows, either other signed-in players or only you.
  • Birth month and year: you, and staff. Other players see only an age group.
  • Gender division: not shown to other players on your profile. You see it in your own profile. But a rating in men's or women's doubles shows which division you play in.
  • Legal name: you, and staff.
  • Email address: you, and staff. Never other players.
  • Your password: nobody. We store only a hash that can't be turned back into it.
  • Whether you confirmed or disputed a match: players in the match see whether someone on each team confirmed it, and whether it is disputed. In singles, or when your partner hasn't confirmed, this can show what you did. You see your own latest answer. Other players see neither. Staff whose role needs it can see your answer.
  • Your privacy settings: you, and staff whose role needs them.
  • When you joined and last signed in: staff whose role needs it.
  • Your sign-in session: nobody, through the app.
  • The audit record of changes: staff whose role needs it. You will get the records about you in the copy of your data we send you.
  • Organizer details: staff.
  • Ratings: no OPEN rating is published yet. Once ratings are published, your display name and current ratings will be visible to every signed-in player, and you won't be able to hide them.

Partner apps

This applies once player accounts open. Approved partner apps, such as club or tournament software, may send in match results for a club that has allowed them to. We don't share player data with partners today. We will update this policy before we do. We will never share the identity or rating of a player aged 13 to 17 with a partner.

Services we use

These companies handle data for us, only to run OPEN:

  • GitHub hosts this website. Privacy statement (GitHub, opens in a new tab)
  • Tally runs the "Get involved" form. Privacy policy (Tally, opens in a new tab)
  • DigitalOcean will run the servers and the database behind player accounts. Privacy policy (DigitalOcean, opens in a new tab)
  • Resend will send account email. It keeps a copy of each message for up to 30 days. We keep its open and click tracking turned off. Privacy policy (Resend, opens in a new tab)

These companies may handle your data in the United States, or in other countries where they run their services.

Photon, the place search at signup, is not on this list. It is a public service that komoot runs under its own privacy policy, not a company that handles data for us. See "Location search at signup" above.

Ages

This applies once player accounts open.

  • You must be 13 or older to have an account.
  • Signup asks for your email address and password first, then your birth month and year. OPEN checks your age before you can type your name or location. If you are under 13, no account is made. We keep a note that signup was refused, on our servers, with that sign-in session. The note is only a flag, never your age or any other signup details. It stays if you log out, and lasts until the session ends.
  • Sometimes the app asks for your birth month and year again after signup, for example if you confirmed your email on another device. If the answer then is under 13, we create no player profile, and keep the same note. Your account, with its email address and password hash, already exists at that point. It has no player profile, so we delete that account if you ask, or when we learn of it.
  • For players aged 13 to 17, the privacy settings start at "only you", and the player can widen them later. Their data is never shared with partners until they turn 18.

What we never ask for

  • Payment details. OPEN never charges anyone.
  • Your street address, postcode or exact location.
  • Your full birth date.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your gender identity. We only ask which division you play in.

How long we keep it

This applies once player accounts open.

  • We keep your account details while your account exists. We don't delete accounts for being inactive.
  • Deleting an account is not built yet. Today, an account with a player profile can't be deleted at all. Until deletion is built, email info@openpb.org and we will deactivate your account. A deactivated account keeps all its details until full deletion is available. While it is deactivated, other players see "Private player" in its matches, and player search leaves it out.
  • Deleting an account won't remove everything, because other players' records and ratings depend on some of it. These stay: match results in the Ledger, OPEN's permanent record of matches; your player profile's OPEN Code; the audit record of changes, which never holds your name, email address or age; if you verified an organizer as staff, the record that you did; and, if you have submitted a match or made a change that OPEN records, such as disputing a match or changing a setting, your account itself, deactivated, with your identifying details removed. These go: your email address, password hash, display name and legal name, birth month and year, gender division, city and region, privacy settings, sign-in sessions, your organizer memberships and club roles, the name of an organizer that is you, and the admin history entries tied to your account. We are still deciding how deletion treats your confirmations and disputes, the link to matches you submitted, the events of an organizer that is one person, whether the join and last sign-in dates on a kept account are reset, what your player profile's link to your account becomes, and whether an account that never did any of that is deleted outright. Until then they stay. We will update this policy before full deletion is available.
  • We delete each account email from our outbox once it is sent, and never keep one more than three days. Resend's copy ages out within 30 days. Deleting an account can't remove that copy sooner.

Getting a copy of your data

This applies once player accounts open. You can always have a copy of your data. A button to download it is not built yet. Until it is, email info@openpb.org and we will help.

Keeping your data safe

  • openpb.org uses HTTPS, and so will the OPEN app.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • The links we email you to confirm your address or reset your password are signed, so we keep no separate list of them. The email itself waits in our outbox until it is sent, three days at most, and Resend keeps a copy for up to 30 days.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a security problem, please tell us at info@openpb.org.

Your choices

This applies once player accounts open.

  • Your privacy settings choose who sees your own match history, stats, rating history, home club, and city and region: other signed-in players, or only you. They start at "signed-in players", or at "only you" for players aged 13 to 17.
  • OPEN doesn't show other players your stats, rating history or home club yet. Your settings for them will apply once it does.
  • They don't hide you in other players' match histories. A match you played names you there, on their settings.
  • Change your details in your profile.
  • Ask us anything about your data at info@openpb.org.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we post the new version here with a new version number and date. If you have an account, we will email you before a change that matters to you takes effect.
