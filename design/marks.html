Supporting assets · the middle ground

Rating ruler

The 2.0 to 8.0 scale as a hairline ruler with players as dots. It works as a divider, a hero accent, and a loading state, and it carries "statistics" without any color.

Distribution curve

The ball's crest from the teaser image, redrawn as a bell curve of ratings. A filled dot marks one player. It ties the planet-like horizon to the idea of a population of ratings.

Hole field

The ball's hole pattern as a dot field that fades out. A quiet texture for section breaks, empty states, and the edges of cards. Rows can be read as a scatter plot.

Old emblem, monochrome

The about page's emblem kept as line work only: no pine, no optic yellow. Useful if the court-and-ball idea should live on as an illustration rather than the logo.
