01 · What we stand for

A rating that belongs to the players

OPEN, the Open Pickleball Evaluation Network, is a free pickleball rating system driven by the community instead of a company. Here's what we're building it on.

  • Free

    No subscriptions, paid upgrades, or ads. Players are never asked for money, in the app or at events.

  • Transparent

    The rules behind every rating will be public. You'll be able to see how your number was worked out, and question it when it looks wrong.

  • Community-driven

    No company, sponsor, or individual will ever own OPEN, and sponsors get no say over ratings. Your ratings and match history are yours to download any time.

Notes.
  • Three pillars instead of five. Each one has a single title. Portable is folded into Community-driven as the last sentence. One standard is dropped, and with it the line saying OPEN works alongside the ratings you already have. That line reassures players, so it could come back in the intro if you want it.
  • "Rating system" is the usual way to say it. DUPR and UTR both call themselves rating systems, and it fixes the cut-off feeling of "a free pickleball rating".
  • Removed: the line linking the mission charter. It sent visitors to GitHub. The "Just getting started" status line is gone too. Get involved still says OPEN is at the very beginning.
  • Still removed: "open-source", the governance status block, and the 2.0–8.0 ruler and scale line.

02 · Get involved

Be part of it from the start

OPEN is at the very beginning: no app and no ratings yet, just volunteers and a set of promises. Want a say in what OPEN becomes, or just want to follow along? We'd love to hear from you.

Notes.
  • Just a call to action. The card, the role list and the fine print are gone. The form explains itself once it opens.
  • One way in. The button opens your Tally form (Kp50eD) as a popup. GitHub appears only in the footer, so players never have to choose between two forms.
  • When we build it, the button will also link to tally.so/r/Kp50eD so it still works if the popup script doesn't load.
