The ball, UTR-simple

UTR's mark is one solid ball with one idea in it. Here the idea is the holes of an indoor pickleball: 26 large holes, laid out as a pole, a ring of 6, a band of 12 round the middle, another 6 and the other pole. The flat options keep only the front face. The sphere options project the real layout, so holes near the edge squash into slivers and give the ball depth. The green O is set aside for now.
