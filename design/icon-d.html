D, with the spin marks back

Round 3's D (the 26-hole indoor ball, seen straight down the pole) inside the spin marks from today's logo. The marks are redrawn to follow a round ball instead of the wider Krona O, keeping the same three segments (0.30 : 0.27 : 0.29 of the arc, even gaps) and the same taper from a point to a flat end. The ball shrinks to make room, so the holes get a little bigger to stay readable.
